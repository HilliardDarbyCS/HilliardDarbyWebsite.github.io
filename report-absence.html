Report an absence

This prototype does not submit attendance reports. Report actual absences through the ParentSquare app. Nothing entered below is sent to the school.

Report through ParentSquare

  1. Open your ParentSquare app using your family account.
  2. Select Attendance on the home screen.
  3. Complete the absence report and follow ParentSquare’s submission steps.

On a computer, sign in to ParentSquare and locate Attendance. The district describes it in the navigation under Communicate; placement may vary.

Report an absence ahead of time when possible. Hilliard replaced SchoolMessenger/Safe Arrival with ParentSquare on August 1, 2025.
