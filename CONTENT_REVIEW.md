# Content review

This is a presentation prototype using information retrieved from the
official Darby and district websites on September 14, 2026.

## Included

- Main school navigation and school section pages.
- Current retrieved school leadership and counseling assignments.
- Regular and two-hour-delay bell schedules.
- Location, office contact information, and building hours.
- Arts programs, retrieved staff names, and published theater season dates.
- Parent and student resource directories.
- Listed Darby sports, with separate team landing pages.
- Selected homepage news summaries linking to original articles.
- Academic Clubs placeholder.

## Content still maintained at the original source

- Full articles, staff biographies and staff-directory embeds.
- Linked PDFs and documents.
- Dynamic calendars and announcements after the review date.
- Team schedules, results, rosters and coach details.
- Full military-family resource descriptions and detailed support contacts.
- Complete course descriptions, prerequisites and participation policies.
- Galleries, media recordings and additional historical content.

These are not represented as fully migrated.

## Retrieval gaps

Some individual parent-directory documents could not be retrieved.
Their cards explicitly direct visitors to the original parent directory.

Some school-linked documents have older labels, including the parking
form, bus lineup map and athletic physical form. Dates are identified
where known. Confirm current versions with the school.

## Images

Every generated page has a different local SVG image placeholder.
These are labeled layout placeholders, not photographs.

Replace each placeholder with an appropriate school-approved image.
No photographs were copied from Instagram.

## Technical verification

This generator and website were not executed or browser-tested in the
ChatGPT session. Review desktop and mobile layouts, keyboard navigation,
links, and page content before presenting or publishing.
